# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We strive to create a productive, respectful, and technically focused community for this project. Our goal is to build high-quality software through good collaboration, honest feedback, and merit-based contributions.

## Our Standards

**Expected Behavior:**

- Focus on technical matters and the project's goals
- Provide constructive, specific, and respectful feedback
- Assume good faith and communicate clearly
- Value contributions based on their quality and usefulness
- Keep discussions professional and on-topic
- Be open to differing technical opinions while staying civil

**Unacceptable Behavior:**

- Personal attacks, insults, or targeted harassment
- Derailing technical discussions with off-topic activism, politics, or unrelated social issues
- Trolling, spamming, or deliberate disruption
- Attempts to shift the project toward non-technical goals

Project spaces (issues, PRs, discussions, official chat) are for development. Casual or off-topic conversation should stay in clearly designated areas, if any.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for enforcing this Code of Conduct.

- Minor issues will usually result in a warning and request to adjust behavior.
- Serious or repeated violations (anything deemed unacceptable) may lead to content removal, temporary blocks, or bans.
- We prioritize healthy discussions and arguments over feelings.

If you experience or observe problematic behavior, please report it to the maintainers (contact info in the README). All reports will be reviewed seriously and handled confidentially where appropriate. Retaliation against good-faith reporters is not tolerated.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all official project spaces and when representing the project publicly.
