What are Bezels?

Retro games were designed for 4:3 or other non-widescreen displays. On modern monitors, this leaves large black bars on the sides. Bezels are high-quality image overlays that frame the game screen with authentic arcade art, console borders, or thematic decorations.

Bezels transform your display from a simple window into a virtual arcade cabinet or a handheld console screen.

Using the Bezel Browser

TruchiEmu includes a powerful Bezel Browser that allows you to discover and download frames without leaving the application.

1. Open the Bezel Selector

From the Game Detail view or the Player overlay, open the Bezel settings.

2. Browse Assets

Explore the library of available bezels. You can filter by system or search for specific themes.

3. Apply & Download

Select a bezel to apply it instantly. If the asset isn't cached locally, TruchiEmu will download it automatically.

Automatic Mapping

To save you time, TruchiEmu uses an intelligent mapping system. When you launch a game, the app automatically suggests bezels that match:

  • System Type: A NES game will suggest NES-themed bezels.
  • Aspect Ratio: The bezel chosen will match the internal resolution of the core.
  • Game Theme: In some cases, specific bezels are mapped to specific titles for maximum immersion.

Fine-Tuning Your Fit

Because every monitor and resolution is different, TruchiEmu provides tools to ensure your game window fits the bezel perfectly.

Positioning

Adjust the X and Y offset of the game screen to center it exactly within the bezel art.

Scaling

Scale the game window up or down to ensure it doesn't overlap the bezel edges or leave unwanted gaps.

Real-time Preview

All adjustments are reflected instantly, allowing you to pixel-perfect your setup.
